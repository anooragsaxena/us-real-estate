## Explicit model discipline

- **Guarded base**: value change **0% year 1 and 2**, then 1% year 3, 1.5% years 4–10; rent change 0% year 1, 1% year 2 and 1.5% years 3–10. Taxes +3.5% annually, insurance +5.5%, HOA +4%. These are *assumptions*, not econometric forecasts or a promised return.
- **Downside**: property prices -6%, -4%, -2% over first 3 years, then zero years 4–6, slight 0.5% gains in years 7–10. Rent -5% in year 1, flat years 2–3, only 0.5% increases after; vacancy at least one month/year. Insurance +20% level shock and 9% yearly increases, property taxes +5%, HOA +6%.
- **Limited upside**: property prices **0% year 1 and 2**, then only 1.5% year 3 and 2% thereafter; rent 0% year 1, 1.5% year 2 and 2% thereafter. This is a sensitivity, **not** the expected outcome or reason to purchase.
- **Mortgage rates**: existing contracted rate is held **fixed** for 5 and 10 years; no implicit refinancing, cash-out or return to 3%. Starting illustrative example 7.75% if no lender term sheet; test rate sensitivity **6%, 7%, 7.75%, 8.5%** as alternative ORIGINATION rate quotes, not future refi assumptions. The actual foreign-national DSCR loan terms may differ from conforming benchmarks.
- **Inflation and selling costs**: 3% annual CPI assumption for illustrative REAL (inflation-adjusted) IRR only and **6% exit selling expense**. Both configurable; they are **not verified forecasts/quotes**. Model escalates landlord operating expenses separately and faster than rent in base/downside.
- **IRR**: starts with down payment, closing costs, upfront repairs and liquid reserves committed. Yearly rental cash after operations/capex/debt and net sale proceeds (after selling costs, loan payoff, return of original reserves) are cash flows. Ongoing capex reserve provision is an **economic expense**, presumed consumed in long-run replacements; do not count as returned cash at sale. Initial reserves assumed still intact, and any owner-funded cash deficits are separate cash outflows. The initial reserves assumption must be reviewed if deficits are actually funded from those reserves.
- **No accounting magic**: mortgage principal repayment improves exit equity but is not extra annual cash flow. Price trajectory starts at actual purchase price, not a higher Zillow Zestimate or unadjusted comp median. No retroactively imagined gain upon purchase.
- **Investment hurdle**: 8% default nominal discount rate is **just a sensitivity reference**, NOT the investor's approved required return. Compare alternative bonds/treasuries net of tax and local risks before finalizing a project-specific hurdle; no approval gate from this placeholder.
- **Tax and currency**: no US/Canada after-tax cashflow, foreign credits, estate/FIRPTA or CAD/USD modeled. These can materially alter after-tax investor IRR. Do not promote the model to a final buying decision without cross-border tax work.

**Model does not establish a probability of appreciation.** The user prioritizes capital preservation and has explicitly challenged first-year appreciation and reliance on a rate-cycle rescue. Historical or national trend data should inform scenario bounds; evaluate local forward supply and executed rental comps for each property.

Reassess macro evidence at least quarterly and replace assumptions when property-specific primary evidence warrants changes. Document versioned scenarios; do not silently change future-year forecasts to make a property pass.
