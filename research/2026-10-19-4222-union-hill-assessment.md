A lower price **could** compensate for this, but no rent roll/lease evidence establishes the size of any adjustment.
2. **Smaller size/condition differences.** A 1,099-sf home compares with larger 1,150- and 1,280-sf closed examples. Few listing photos and a sparse interior description do not prove renovation is needed; age/condition, roof, HVAC and deferred maintenance require independent inspection.
3. **HOA.** $75/month is a real income drag. Written CC&Rs, lease rules, special assessments, dues history and property insurance scope remain unknown.
4. **Seller price reductions.** The $25K October cut could reflect market exposure, tenant-occupied sales friction, condition or initial overpricing. **Reason not verified.**

This is an evidence-based **cause investigation, not an assertion of a defect**.
